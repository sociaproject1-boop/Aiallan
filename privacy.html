Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aiallan.shop (“Allan,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit or use our website, including optional social-profile connection features.

We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate the website and the features you choose to use.

1. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive:

  • Basic website information: technical information such as browser/device information, approximate location derived from standard network information, pages or features accessed, and security or diagnostic information.
  • Referral information: information such as a referring website or campaign parameters (for example, UTM parameters) that help us understand how visitors reach Allan.
  • Contact information: information you choose to submit through an Allan contact or inquiry form, such as your name, email address, phone number, message, links, and attachments.
  • Social profile information: if you voluntarily connect an eligible social account, Allan may receive information permitted by the provider and the permissions you approve, such as a provider user ID, display name, username, profile image, profile link, follower count, country information when available, and verification status.

2. Social Login and Authorization

Connecting a social account is optional. Allan uses the provider’s official authorization flow rather than attempting to read another application’s private account information from your device.

When you authorize a provider, that provider may share the information and permissions shown on its authorization screen. You can choose not to connect your account. If you do connect, you may revoke access through the applicable provider where that option is available.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • operate, maintain, and improve Aiallan.shop;
  • provide features you request, including displaying permitted social profile information on the “Guests of Allan” area;
  • respond to inquiries and requests;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
  • understand traffic sources and improve the website experience; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Public Guest Profile Display

If you voluntarily connect a social account for the Allan guest-profile feature, selected permitted profile details may be displayed publicly on Aiallan.shop. The displayed information can include your name, username, profile image, profile link, follower count, country information when available, and verification status.

You may request removal of your guest profile through the contact method provided on Aiallan.shop. We may also deactivate a profile when required by a provider, for security reasons, or when the profile no longer meets the feature requirements.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Allan and its service providers may use cookies or similar browser technologies for essential site functions, security, authentication, preferences, and diagnostics. During social-account authorization, temporary security information may also be stored in a browser cookie to help protect the OAuth process against unauthorized requests.

6. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers to host the website, store website data, process authentication or authorization requests, provide infrastructure, and operate third-party integrations. Examples may include hosting/database infrastructure and the social platforms you choose to connect.

We do not sell your social-profile connection information as a standalone data product. We disclose information only as reasonably necessary to operate the requested service, protect the website, comply with law, or fulfill a transaction or request.

7. Social Platform Privacy Policies

When you connect TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or another third-party service, that provider also processes your information under its own privacy policy and terms. Allan does not control the privacy practices of those providers.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining website functionality, responding to requests, meeting legal obligations, and resolving security or dispute issues. Guest-profile information may be deactivated or removed when requested or when it is no longer needed for the feature.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Choices and Requests

You may choose not to connect a social account. If you have connected an account and want your Allan guest profile removed or want to ask about information associated with your use of the site, contact us using the contact method provided on Aiallan.shop. We may need enough information to identify the relevant profile or request while avoiding unnecessary collection of additional personal information.

11. Children

Allan is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so we can review the request.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website, integrations, or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, social-profile removal requests, or other data-related requests, please use the contact method provided on Aiallan.shop.

This Privacy Policy is general informational privacy language for the Allan website and is not legal advice. If your business has specific regulatory, contractual, or jurisdiction-specific requirements, have the policy reviewed by a qualified privacy professional or lawyer.
